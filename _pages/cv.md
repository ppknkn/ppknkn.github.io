<div class="cv-entry__body">
<h3>SparkR — compact wheel-legged robot</h3>
<p class="cv-entry__role">Independent mechanical, electronics & control development</p>
<ul>
<li>Designed a compact wheel-legged mechanism with direct-drive hub motors and built a working platform approximately <strong>20 cm</strong> tall and <strong>2.3 kg</strong> in mass.</li>
<li>Integrated <strong>STM32</strong> embedded control, <strong>1 kHz VMC-LQR</strong> balance control, PID loops, CAN motor communication, IMU attitude estimation and video transmission.</li>
<li>Designed the power-distribution and CAN hub PCBs in EasyEDA, then handled fabrication, soldering, wiring and system tuning.</li>
<li>Prototype performance: average speed <strong>2.2 m/s</strong>, obstacle traversal of approximately <strong>7 cm</strong>, and runtime of <strong>35 minutes</strong>.</li>
</ul>
